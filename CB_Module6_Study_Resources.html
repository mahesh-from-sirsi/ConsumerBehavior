Case studies (6)

Read each fictionalised Indian-market case, answer in teams, then expand the suggested analysis.

Case 1: The Appliance Retailer’s Leaky Funnel

A Shivamogga electronics store spends Rs. 48,000 on local ads, receives 240 enquiries, makes 60 sales and discovers that 20 buyers request help setting up the product. Sales staff want more ad budget; service staff want onboarding support. The owner has only Rs. 20,000 extra this month.

Concepts: Acquisition, CAC, retention, CRM success factors

  1. Calculate acquisition cost per new buyer.
  2. Which funnel and after-sales measures are missing?
  3. How would you allocate the Rs. 20,000 and test success?
Show suggested analysis
  1. CAC = 48,000/60 = Rs. 800 per buyer, assuming all 60 are new and all costs included.
  2. Track qualified leads, gross contribution, return rate, first-use success, complaints, repeat buying and referral.
  3. Pilot onboarding for a subset; compare complaints, returns and repeat visits against a fair comparison group.

Case 2: The SLA That Looked Perfect

A regional internet provider reports 98% SLA compliance because it answers most complaints within one hour. Customers say repairs take three days and many must call twice. The contract only defines first-response time.

Concepts: SLAs, service profiling, customer service

  1. Why can SLA compliance coexist with dissatisfaction?
  2. Draft two additional measurable SLA clauses.
  3. What should be escalated and reported?
Show suggested analysis
  1. The SLA measures acknowledgement, not resolution quality or repeat contact.
  2. Specify restoration targets by severity and periodic status updates with clock rules and exclusions.
  3. Monitor repeat calls, restoration time, root causes and clear breach communication.

Case 3: Cross-Sell or Mis-Sell?

A bank relationship executive is rewarded for selling insurance to savings-account customers. A retired customer says they only want a fixed deposit. The executive suggests an insurance-linked product without explaining lock-in or risks.

Concepts: Cross-selling, customer value, ethics

  1. Is this a valid cross-sell?
  2. How should incentives and scripts change?
  3. What customer outcome should be tracked?
Show suggested analysis
  1. No: suitability and informed consent are missing.
  2. Separate needs assessment from incentive pressure; disclose risks, exclusions and alternatives.
  3. Track complaint rates, suitability checks, cancellations and long-term satisfaction, not just product count.

Case 4: The Tuition Centre’s Churn Prediction

A coaching centre has 300 learners. Its spreadsheet flags anyone absent twice as likely to leave. Staff begin sending repeated promotional messages. Some absences are caused by exams or illness, and parents complain.

Concepts: Prediction, retention, event-based marketing

  1. What is wrong with the prediction rule?
  2. Propose a better service-oriented intervention.
  3. How would you evaluate accuracy and fairness?
Show suggested analysis
  1. Absence is ambiguous; the rule has false positives and lacks validation.
  2. Offer a single respectful check-in through a permitted channel, with an easy opt-out.
  3. Compare actual dropout outcomes, false-positive rates, complaints and different learner circumstances.

Case 5: The Call Centre Script Versus Reality

A mixer-grinder company instructs agents to finish calls within four minutes. A customer reports a burning smell; the agent follows the standard troubleshooting script and tells them to run the machine again. The complaint escalates online.

Concepts: Call scripting, service recovery, safety, KPIs

  1. Which failure is most serious?
  2. Rewrite the script opening and escalation.
  3. Which metrics should change?
Show suggested analysis
  1. Potential safety risk: the agent should not suggest continued use of a potentially hazardous appliance.
  2. Advise customer to stop using the product and follow company safety procedures; escalate promptly, document and arrange inspection.
  3. Balance handling time with safety escalation, resolution quality, repeat contacts and CSAT.

Case 6: The Renewal Reminder Experiment

A local water-purifier service provider has 400 customers due for annual service. Half receive a consented reminder; half are assigned to a no-reminder comparison group. Bookings are 90 in the reminder group and 64 in the comparison group.

Concepts: Event marketing, channel optimisation, incremental lift

  1. Calculate booking rates and absolute lift.
  2. What assumptions make this a fair test?
  3. What other metrics matter?
Show suggested analysis
  1. Rates: 45% versus 32%; absolute lift 13 percentage points, 26 additional bookings among 200 contacted.
  2. Groups should be comparable and assigned fairly; check whether other campaigns differed.
  3. Measure service margin, opt-outs, complaints, repeat service and delivery cost.

  1. CRM foundations Define CRM and explain operational, analytical and collaborative CRM with examples.
  2. CRM foundations Explain the customer lifecycle and customer acquisition process.
  3. CRM foundations Discuss CRM success factors and causes of implementation failure.
  4. Loyalty Differentiate satisfaction, retention, behavioural loyalty and attitudinal loyalty.
  5. Loyalty Explain how a company can optimise customer relationships without excessive promotional contact.
  6. Profiling Explain three instructional levels of service/sales profiling with examples.
  7. SLA Define an SLA and explain its essential elements and benefits.
  8. SLA Design an SLA for a regional appliance service provider with measurable targets.
  9. SLA Differentiate an SLA from a KPI and illustrate service escalation.
  10. Marketing CRM Explain CRM applications in marketing and one-to-one relationship marketing.
  11. Marketing CRM Discuss segmentation, RFM and personalisation in a retail CRM programme.
  12. Selling Differentiate cross-selling, up-selling and bundling with suitable examples.
  13. Retention Explain customer retention strategies and calculate retention rate with an example.
  14. Retention Discuss cohort analysis and churn measurement.
  15. Prediction Explain behavioural prediction and the limitations of churn models.
  16. Profitability Define customer profitability and discuss attributable costs.
  17. Value modelling Explain customer lifetime value and solve a simple CLV problem.
  18. Channel What is channel optimisation? Explain the criteria for selecting channels.
  19. Event marketing Define event-based marketing and design a trigger-based service reminder.
  20. Customer service Explain the role of call centres and important performance measures.
  21. Call scripting Draft a customer complaint call script and explain escalation.
  22. Satisfaction Explain CSAT, NPS and CES with measurement examples.
  23. Case application A company meets response-time SLAs but has low CSAT. Diagnose and recommend changes.
  24. Case application Design a CRM dashboard for a local retailer covering acquisition, service, retention and customer value.
