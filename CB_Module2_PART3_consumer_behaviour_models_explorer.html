Compare the models

Choose a model based on the question you want to investigate.

ModelBest suited to exploreUseful questionImportant limitation
IOP / IPOA broad input–process–outcome viewWhat influences the decision, what happens, and what results?Broad framework; detail depends on the version being used.
NicosiaCompany communication and consumer responseHow might a firm's message relate to attitude, search, purchase and feedback?May not capture every buying route equally well, such as routine or primarily habit-driven purchases.
EKBDecision stages and influences along a consumer journeyWhere might a buyer need information or leave the purchase process?Real decisions can be shortened, repeated, skipped or non-linear.
Howard–ShethPerception, learning, motives and brand choiceWhy might two buyers respond differently to similar stimuli?Detailed and complex; requires care to operationalise and measure its constructs.
