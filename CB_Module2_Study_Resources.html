Case studies

The laptop decision journey

Instructional scenario based on Module 2 notes

An MBA student needs a laptop for assignments, reads reviews, compares warranty and RAM, buys an available model online and later discovers poor battery life.

Discussion questions

  1. Map each event to the five buying-decision stages.
  2. Identify possible post-purchase dissonance.
  3. Suggest two actions the seller could take.
Reveal suggested discussion points
  1. Need recognition → information search → evaluation → purchase → post-purchase evaluation.
  2. The student may regret choosing the available model.
  3. Accurate product descriptions and responsive support or return options reduce dissatisfaction.

Checkout abandonment at an online store

Instructional scenario based on Module 2 notes

A shopper compares two backpacks online, adds one to the cart, then abandons checkout after unexpected shipping charges appear.

Discussion questions

  1. Which situational and digital factors influenced the decision?
  2. How should the retailer test possible improvements?
Reveal suggested discussion points
  1. Unexpected cost at checkout altered the purchase decision.
  2. Test upfront total-price disclosure and a simpler checkout; monitor abandonment and conversion rates.
